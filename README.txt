MUHAMMAD ANAS — PERSONAL PORTFOLIO

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html in your browser (Live Server is recommended).

PAGES
- index.html: Home and social links
- about.html: Bio and education overview
- education.html: Full academic history
- skills.html: Skills and tools
- projects.html: Project overview
- photography.html: Photography portfolio
- video-editing.html: Video editing portfolio
- contact.html: Contact details and email form

FILES
- css/style.css: All styling and responsive layout
- js/main.js: Mobile navigation, reveal animations, and email form

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace the Instagram and Facebook placeholder links with your profile URLs.
- Replace the photography and video editing decorative placeholders with your own work.
- Add real project descriptions and links when your projects are ready to share.
- Contact form opens the visitor's default email app; it does not send messages through a server.
- WhatsApp chat link opens a conversation at +92 337 6268645.
- Google Fonts requires an internet connection; fallback fonts will still work offline.
